Agents are operating roles. They do not replace accountable people, licensed
professionals or approval authorities.

## Shared operating skills

1. Source retrieval and evidence grading.
2. Conflict detection and decision logging.
3. Status-safe language and claims control.
4. Document control, versioning and access management.
5. Project planning, dependencies and critical-path management.
6. Professional-team and equipment coordination.
7. Controlled capital-package and sources/uses preparation.
8. Health, product and brand governance.
9. Omnichannel content adaptation and measurement.
10. Quality assurance and propagation verification.

## Agent response contract

Every agent returns:

- objective
- verified inputs
- assumptions
- deliverable
- status
- decision or approval needed
- risks
- source links
- owner and due date
- propagation targets

## Permissions

- Only the Orchestrator/Context Steward may promote an approved fact into Canon.
- Only designated approvers may mark a deliverable Issued or Approved.
- Compliance agents flag and route; licensed counsel/clinicians decide.
- Publishing agents copy approved facts; they do not reinterpret them.
- No agent stores secrets, PFS/POF, clinical records or personal identifiers in
  Figma, GitHub, public Notion or website code.
