# Status, risk and decision controls

## Status vocabulary

- **CONCEPT:** exploratory; no execution representation.
- **R&D:** development/testing; not approved product.
- **PRE-DEVELOPMENT:** site/program work underway; not permitted/open.
- **DRAFT:** incomplete and not approved.
- **REVIEW:** ready for designated review.
- **APPROVED:** approved for named internal use.
- **ISSUED:** released to the identified audience.
- **SUPERSEDED:** replaced; retained for history.
- **ARCHIVE:** inactive historical material.

## Public decision protocol

This repository records the method for governing decisions, not the confidential
decision register. Each material decision must have a stable ID, date, owner,
status, source reference and supersession history in the controlled system of
record. Public documentation may include only an approved summary after privacy,
commercial, legal and claims review.

The controlled register is maintained outside public GitHub. It covers portfolio
governance, product canon, property diligence, capital readiness, communications,
brand approvals and any unresolved conflicts. Exact economics, stakeholder data,
contact routing and approval evidence must never be copied here.

## Risk rating

Rating = probability (1–5) × impact (1–5).

- 15–25: red; weekly executive review.
- 8–14: amber; named mitigation and due date.
- 1–7: green; monitor.
